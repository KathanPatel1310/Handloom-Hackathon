# Data Dictionary — Handloom Demand Forecasting Dataset

Every column below is tagged so the methodology can be defended to judges:

- **REAL** — actual named real-world entities (cluster names, states, specialties)
- **REAL-PROXY** — a real, freely available public signal used as an input
- **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** — generated by a documented mechanism grounded in real
  patterns (seasonality, festival timing, price elasticity), not random noise
- **SYNTHETIC-PLACEHOLDER** — a stand-in series that MUST be replaced with a real
  source before final submission if time allows; the script tells you exactly
  where to plug the real series in

## demand_weekly.csv — CORE MODELING TABLE (synthetic, mechanistically generated)
`units_ordered` = base_level(cluster,product) × seasonal × festival_multiplier ×
wedding_multiplier × cotton_price_elasticity × cluster_trend + Poisson noise.
Every coefficient is a named constant at the top of `generate_dataset.py` with
a one-line justification — nothing is a buried magic number.

Validation (printed by the script, re-check after any edits):
- Demand lift near major festivals vs normal weeks: **~51%**
- Correlation of total weekly demand with google_trends_index: **0.82**

## orders_log.csv — order-level disaggregation
Directly answers the two parts of the problem statement most teams will skip:
**"lack of visibility into future orders"** (promised vs actual delivery dates)
and **"delayed payments"** (payment_due_date vs payment_received_date). In this
run, average payment delay vs due date is **9 days**, with **60%** of orders
paid at least somewhat late — a realistic mix, not "everyone is late."

## weaver_cashflow_weekly.csv — income stability & financial inclusion layer
This table converts demand + payment timing into **income accrual vs cash-in**,
plus receivables and a credit-stress flag. It is designed so your app can show:
*“Next 4 weeks cash-in forecast and whether I should borrow / slow production.”*

| Column | Tag | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| cluster_id / cluster_name / state / week_start_date | — / **REAL** | join keys and real entities |
| units_ordered / units_delivered | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | aggregated from `demand_weekly.csv` |
| gross_merchandise_value_inr | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | ordered_units × avg price (illustrative GMV) |
| delivered_revenue_inr | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | sum(quantity × unit_price) by actual delivery week |
| cash_in_inr | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | sum(quantity × unit_price) by payment received week |
| raw_material_cost_inr / wage_cost_inr / loom_maintenance_cost_inr | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | simple cost model driven by material + CPI |
| working_capital_drag_inr | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | small weekly cost of carrying receivables |
| net_income_accrual_inr | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | revenue − costs (income when work is delivered) |
| net_cashflow_inr | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | cash_in − costs − working-capital drag |
| active_weavers_est | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | implied from delivered units & productivity assumptions |
| income_per_active_weaver_inr | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | per-weaver weekly income estimate |
| pending_receivables_inr | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | cumulative delivered revenue − cumulative cash-in |
| receivable_weeks_outstanding | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | pending_receivables / (4w avg revenue) |
| income_volatility_score | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | capped rolling volatility metric |
| credit_need_flag | **SYNTHETIC-CALIBRATED** | True if sustained negative cashflow or receivables > ~8 weeks |
